# Naming — medical-learning-intelligence

Working log of brand name candidates for the platform (NEET-PG/INICET exam prep + enterprise/CME
"topic mastery" track). Repo name stays `medical-learning-intelligence` until a final name is
chosen and rebrand happens.

## Context / requirements
- Two product tracks: (1) competitive exam prep, individual students (B2C); (2) topic
  mastery / CME for institutions, hospitals (B2B, enterprise)
- AI-powered (question bank, AI tutor, spaced repetition, performance analytics)
- Primary market: India (NEET-PG, INICET, FMGE/NExT), expanding to global licensing exams later
- Wanted: profession visibly signaled ("Doc"/"Med"), easy to say and spell on first hearing,
  distinctive, not generic
- Sub-brand pattern once parent is chosen: `[Parent] Path`, `[Parent] Ortho`, `[Parent] Cardio`,
  `[Parent] Neuro`, `[Parent] Enterprise`

## Strong candidates (no collision found, still live options)

### DocEdge ⭐ current front-runner
- No exam-prep/med-ed collision found
- One unrelated hit: DocEdge® Communicator — a clinical documentation/CDI SaaS tool (hospital
  workflow, not exam prep) — different category, low confusion risk, but confirm trademark class
  before filing
- "Doc" = visible profession; "Edge" = competitive advantage, fits percentile/rank positioning
- Passes pronunciation filter (2 syllables, phonetic)
- Note: "Doc" can mean "document" out of context — minor risk, resolves quickly once seen next to
  med-ed branding/sub-brands

### DocIQ
- No exam-prep/med-ed collision found
- Adjacent pattern already proven: NurseiQ (nursing exam prep app) uses same "[role]+IQ" formula
- "IQ" signals AI/intelligence without literally saying "AI" (avoids dating the name)
- Passes pronunciation filter
- Same minor "Doc = document" ambiguity as DocEdge

### Zenith
- No collision found anywhere in med-ed
- Real, universally known word — zero ambiguity spoken or spelled
- Doesn't visibly signal "medical" — would need a tagline to carry that
- Feedback so far: "looks bad" / not exciting enough

### Kestrel
- No collision found
- Precision/sharp-focus metaphor fits diagnostic + exam-mastery positioning
- Doesn't visibly signal "medical"
- Pronunciation ambiguity risk (kess-trel / kestrel / kestrall) flagged as a word-of-mouth weakness

## Rejected — real collisions found (do not reuse)
All of these had live, often funded, competing products in medical education, exam prep, or
adjacent healthcare tech:

- MedForge, MedVantage, MedNexa, MedAxis (naming pattern only, not individually checked)
- MedPulse — live AI community + MDCAT prep app (Pakistan) + UK/Caribbean CME brands
- MedVital / MedVitals — funded Indian med-device co. ($985K raised, Gurugram) + 3 unrelated others
- DocPulse — established Indian HMS/clinic software co. (est. 2012, Bengaluru)
- DocRank — live healthcare marketing SaaS (docrank.ai)
- DocPlus / Doc+ — live doctor/patient apps (multiple) + Russian telemedicine co.
- DocScore / DocScores — physician ratings platforms (NRC Health, HealthTap)
- DocLumen — live AI health analytics platform, 25,000+ users
- Lumen / MedLumen / Lumen Path / Lumen Innovations — MedLumen is a direct competitor: AI tutor,
  spaced repetition, ranking, 50,000+ users, covers PLAB/USMLE/MRCP/FMGE/SMLE
- Mirai — 5+ live ed-tech products, several Indian, incl. "Mirai Academy" (competitive exam coaching)
- MedoPlus — funded Indian healthtech co. ($1.51M raised), HBR case study, 280k+ users
- MedRise — near-exact match: AI-powered med exam prep + residency coaching, tagline "Score
  higher... Rise faster" with "MedRiseGPT" AI tutor
- MedEdge — live resident learning platform (video courses + exam prep)
- MEDx / Medx QBank — live exam prep for MRCOG/MRCPI/EBCOG/PLAB/MRCP/FCPS/MRCS
- MedMentor — live NEET-PG-specific learning platform (India) + separate Google Play app
- DocPrime — PolicyBazaar Group healthcare venture (major funded Indian co.)
- MedRank — live UK medical trainee rotation-ranking platform + 2 unrelated healthcare SEO/QA cos.
- Lumi / LumiLearn / D2L Lumi / Learn With Lumi / Lumi AI — saturated: 6+ live AI study/exam-prep
  products
- LUMEI — spelling itself is clear, but phonetically identical to "Lumi" (saturated, see above) —
  risky for word-of-mouth
- DEMI (DocEdge Medical Intelligence, shortened) — no direct collision, but heavy generic noise
  (celebrity name, "half" meaning) makes early search discovery hard
- PrepVital — no exact match, but built from the two most generic words in the category
  ("Prep" + "-Vital(s)"), sounds like a knockoff of BoardVitals
- MedPrep — too generic, near-certain to already be in informal use (coaching academies, etc.)
- "med-exam-prepEdge" — not a brand name, literal description, fails pronunciation test

## Also considered, not pursued further
- Sanskrit/Tamil-rooted names (Medha, Arivu, Prajna, Vidya, etc.) — rejected, wanted English only
- Clinical-metaphor names in the style of "Marrow" (Plexus, Vertex, Cusp, Lumen, Cortex, Sulcus,
  Synapse, etc.) — Lumen ruled out (see Lumen above); others untested but open if revisited
- "Next"-based names — avoid: NExT is the actual official Indian national licensing exam name
  (replacing NEET-PG/FMGE), risk of confusing brand with the exam itself
